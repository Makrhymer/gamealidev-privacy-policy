Privacy Policy for World Food Tour

Last updated: September 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Gamealidev (Muhammad Ali Khan) ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the mobile game World Food Tour (the "App").

We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about the information collected through the App.

1. Information We Collect

World Food Tour does not directly require you to provide personal information such as your name, email address, telephone number, or physical address.

However, the App uses third-party services that may automatically collect certain information from your device and your use of the App.

These services are:

  • Unity Ads
  • GameAnalytics

Information collected by these services may include device information, advertising identifiers, approximate location, app usage information, gameplay events, session information, and information about interactions with advertisements.

2. GameAnalytics

We use GameAnalytics to understand how players use World Food Tour and to improve the game.

GameAnalytics may process information such as:

  • Game sessions and session duration
  • Gameplay events and interactions
  • Progress through the game
  • Device and technical information
  • Random or pseudonymous identifiers
  • In-game activity and gameplay-related metrics

This information is used for analytics, troubleshooting, understanding game performance, and improving the App.

GameAnalytics processes data as an analytics service provider.

For more information about GameAnalytics and its privacy practices, please visit:

GameAnalytics Privacy FAQ

3. Unity Ads

World Food Tour uses Unity Ads to display rewarded advertisements. Rewarded advertisements are optional advertisements that players may choose to watch in exchange for an in-game reward.

Unity Ads may collect and process certain information to provide, measure, and improve advertising services.

This may include:

  • Advertising or device identifiers
  • Approximate location
  • Device and technical information
  • Information about interactions with advertisements
  • Information about ad impressions and performance
  • App usage information relevant to advertising

Unity Ads may share certain information with advertising and technology partners for purposes including advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and compliance.

For more information about Unity's privacy practices, please visit:

Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy

4. How We Use Information

Information collected through the third-party services described above may be used to:

  • Operate and maintain World Food Tour
  • Understand how players use the game
  • Improve gameplay and game performance
  • Diagnose technical problems
  • Measure advertising performance
  • Provide rewarded advertisements
  • Prevent fraud and abuse
  • Maintain the security and integrity of the App
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements

We do not use the information collected through the App to directly identify individual users.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be transmitted to and processed by third-party service providers that help us operate and improve World Food Tour, including GameAnalytics and Unity Ads.

These providers may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies and applicable data protection requirements.

6. Data Security

Information transmitted by the third-party services used by World Food Tour is generally transmitted using encrypted connections where supported by those services.

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with the App. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

GameAnalytics and Unity Ads may retain information according to their respective policies and applicable requirements.

Because World Food Tour does not maintain a user account containing personally identifiable information, we do not maintain a separate personal account database for users of the App.

If you would like to request deletion of information associated with your use of World Food Tour, you may contact us using the contact information below. We will review and process requests as required by applicable law and, where applicable, coordinate with our third-party service providers.

8. Children's Privacy

World Food Tour is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed toward children.

The App does not knowingly request or collect personal information such as names, email addresses, telephone numbers, or physical addresses from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.

9. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict certain processing of your information.

You may also be able to control certain advertising-related settings through your Android device's privacy and advertising settings.

10. Third-Party Privacy Policies

The App uses third-party services whose handling of information is governed by their respective policies.

  • GameAnalytics: GameAnalytics Privacy FAQ
  • Unity: Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy

We encourage users to review these policies for additional information about how these services handle data.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when the App's functionality or the third-party services used by the App change.

Any updated version will be published at the same privacy-policy URL and will include a revised "Last updated" date.
