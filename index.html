# Privacy Policy for World Food Tour **Last updated: September 2, 2026** This Privacy Policy explains how **Gamealidev (Muhammad Ali Khan)** ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the mobile game **World Food Tour** ("the App"). We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about the information collected through the App. ## 1. Information We Collect World Food Tour does not directly require you to provide personal information such as your name, email address, telephone number, or physical address. However, the App uses third-party services that may automatically collect certain information from your device and your use of the App. These services are: * Unity Ads * GameAnalytics The information collected may include device information, advertising identifiers, approximate location, app usage information, gameplay events, session information, and information about interactions with advertisements. ## 2. GameAnalytics We use **GameAnalytics** to understand how players use World Food Tour and to improve the game. GameAnalytics may process information such as: * Game sessions and session duration * Gameplay events and interactions * Progress through the game * Device and technical information * Random or pseudonymous identifiers * In-game activity and other gameplay-related metrics This information is used for analytics, troubleshooting, understanding game performance, and improving the App. GameAnalytics processes data on behalf of the game developer as an analytics service provider. For more information, please see GameAnalytics' privacy information: https://www.gameanalytics.com/trust/privacy-faq ## 3. Unity Ads World Food Tour uses **Unity Ads** to display rewarded advertisements. When you choose to watch a rewarded advertisement, Unity Ads may collect and process certain information to provide, measure, and improve advertising services. This may include: * Advertising or device identifiers * Approximate location * Device and technical information * Information about interactions with advertisements * Information about ad impressions and performance * App usage information relevant to advertising Unity Ads may share certain information with advertising and technology partners for purposes including advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and compliance. For more information about Unity's privacy practices, please see: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy ## 4. How We Use Information Information collected through the third-party services described above may be used to: * Operate and maintain World Food Tour * Understand how players use the game * Improve gameplay and game performance * Diagnose technical problems * Measure advertising performance * Provide rewarded advertisements * Prevent fraud and abuse * Maintain the security and integrity of the App * Comply with applicable legal requirements We do not use the information collected through the App to personally identify you. ## 5. Data Sharing We do not sell your personal information. Information may be transmitted to and processed by third-party service providers that help us operate and improve World Food Tour, including GameAnalytics and Unity Ads. These providers may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies and applicable data protection requirements. ## 6. Data Security Information transmitted by the third-party services used by World Food Tour is generally transmitted using encrypted connections where supported by those services. We will review and process requests as required by applicable law and, where applicable, coordinate with our third-party service providers. ## 8.
